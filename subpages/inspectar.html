inspectAR

History

InspectAR was created by 5 guys. Four of them were from Memorial University in St. John's Newfoundland. They went through Y Combinator and got funding to make this product. The CEO had ties to a microchip manufacturing company. They were working on this for 5 years before I joined. There was a top notch marketing intern and a full time employee working on the file parser.

I joined, having found a posting on Indeed looking for a Unity Developer.

Absolutely excellent work environment, including the downtown office on Water Street overlooking the St. John's harbor. Good quality standing desks, lovely erognomic chairs, and just really great coworkers.

We worked with a dozen interns over the following couple years: Krushnaraj, Abdul, Andrew, Tao, Shuaishuai, Jarret, Priyanka, Sandeep, Jacob, Harsh, Anna, Josh. In March 2026, I was trying to record all the relevant details to an internal wiki and was caught by how many people I personally spent time with who had influence on the product. It was the first time that even mattered.

At different times I worked as a direct report to the CTO, then as part of a team of three, then as a solo client dev, and again briefly with a dedicated report.

A one-of-a-kind product with a one-of-a-kind team. I'll miss them.
